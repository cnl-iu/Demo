1. Cancellation Policy

All cancellations must be submitted in writing to reservations@beachresort.com and will be acknowledged within 24 hours with a cancellation reference number.

Notice Period Refund Amount Processing Time
14 or more days before arrival 100% of total paid 5–10 business days
7 to 13 days before arrival 50% of total paid 5–10 business days
Less than 7 days before arrival 0% — no refund N/A
Peak season (Dec 15 – Jan 5, Easter week) Special terms apply (see peak rates) —

2. Modifications

Reservation modifications (date changes, villa type upgrades) are subject to availability and must be requested at least 7 days prior to arrival. Date changes to a later period are accommodated free of charge once per booking. Rate differences for upgraded villas will be charged at the prevailing rate.

3. Deposit & Payment Schedule

  • At Booking: 50% deposit required within 48 hours of confirmation
  • 14 Days Prior: Remaining 50% balance due
  • Late Bookings (within 14 days): 100% payment required at time of booking
  • On Arrival: Security deposit of ₱5,000 (refunded on departure)

Failure to pay the deposit within the required timeframe may result in automatic cancellation of the reservation without notice.

4. No-Show Policy

In the event of a no-show (failure to arrive on the check-in date without prior cancellation notice), the full booking amount will be forfeited, and the villa will be released for resale after 6:00 PM on the day of arrival. No refund or partial credit will be issued for no-shows.

5. Minimum Stay Requirements

A minimum stay of 2 nights is required year-round for all villa bookings. During peak season (December 15 through January 5, and Holy Week), a minimum stay of 4 nights applies. These requirements are strictly enforced and cannot be waived.

6. Group Bookings

Bookings of 3 or more villas simultaneously are classified as group bookings. Group bookings require a 50% non-refundable deposit at time of confirmation, with the balance due 30 days before arrival. Different cancellation terms apply to group bookings — contact our reservations team for details.

7. Children Policy

Children under 12 years of age stay free when sharing a villa with parents using existing bedding. Children over 12 are counted as adults for occupancy purposes. A maximum of 2 children (under 12) per villa applies. Baby cots are available on request at no extra charge.

8. Force Majeure

In the event that a stay cannot proceed due to circumstances beyond the Resort's control (typhoon, government travel restrictions, natural disaster), we will offer a full date change with no change fees, or a credit voucher valid for 12 months. Cash refunds in force majeure situations are handled case-by-case.

Questions about our booking policy? Contact reservations@beachresort.com or call +63 912 345 6789.
